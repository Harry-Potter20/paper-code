**Setup.**
- **Model:** ECGFounder 12-lead (MIT), its own `ABNORMAL ECG` output.
- **Threshold:** set so that **10% of matched normal controls** are called abnormal. The raw 0.5 cut flags 48–55% of
  plain normal ECGs, so ECGFounder's 0.5 is not a usable operating point.
- **Cohorts:** labels come from the source datasets, never the model. Green-only = sinus brady, sinus arrhythmia,
  1st-degree AVB, incomplete RBBB, QRS-voltage LVH/high voltage, early repolarisation, junctional escape, wandering
  pacemaker; nothing else.

| cohort | controls flagged | green-only flagged [95% CI] | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| PTB-XL (Germany; n = 1,802 each) | 10% | **33.7% [31.5, 35.8]** | 3.4× |
| Chapman/Ningbo (China; n = 2,000 each) | 10% | **17.9% [16.3, 19.7]** | 1.8× |

**By finding, at the same threshold:**

| finding | PTB-XL | Chapman |
|---|---|---|
| sinus bradycardia | **71.6%** (n = 384) | 18.8% (n = 1,728) |

**Mechanism hint.** On sinus-brady-only PTB-XL ECGs the model's top labels are `SINUS BRADYCARDIA` (332/338),
`ABNORMAL ECG` (331), `MARKED SINUS BRADYCARDIA` (236) and **`NONSPECIFIC ST ABNORMALITY` (124/338)**.
- It assigns ST abnormality to slow sinus rhythm.
- This is exactly the general-population machine-read convention that the athlete criteria reject.
- The model faithfully learned a non-athlete ontology.

**Read.** At a realistic operating point (10% false positives on normal ECGs), ECGFounder calls 18–34% of ECGs
carrying only training-related findings abnormal, and up to 72% for isolated sinus bradycardia. Sinus bradycardia is
the most common athletic ECG finding. In a screening population where green findings are the norm, this is a
false-positive cascade.

**Caveats.**
- These are non-athletes who carry green findings, not athletes.
- One model so far; ECG-FM, HuBERT-ECG and the PTB-XL 1D-ViT are next.
- The PTB-XL vs Chapman difference (sinus brady 72% vs 19%) needs explaining: label-definition thresholds (PTB-XL
  SBRAD vs SNOMED SB, heart-rate cut-offs) or acquisition.
- Heart rate per record should be computed to stratify (< 50 / 50–59 bpm).
