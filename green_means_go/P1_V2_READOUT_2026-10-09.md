## Reading
- Bradycardia (<50 bpm) is the dominant over-call driver: 98-100% flagged by the founder output and general head.
- The athlete-criteria linear head lowers green over-call (PTB-XL 46%->33%) **but only by raising normal over-call**
  (18%->36%; Chapman 33%->65%). Its sens-90 threshold collapses to ~0.02-0.03: at 90% red sensitivity it cannot
  separate red from green/normal. It does not discriminate; it relabels.
- So a frozen-backbone *linear* readout does **not** fix the problem. "The fix lives in the readout" is not supported in
  its linear form. Open: nonlinear head / partial fine-tune, or the representation itself entangles green with red.
